## Background

Blossom-end rot pressure climbs whenever EC pushes past 3.5 and the calcium pathway closes. The block under trial was carrying EC 4.2 from late-stage irrigation salinity — a textbook case for the nitrate-only feeding pattern soluCN and soluNOP are built for.

## Trial setup

- Two equally-sized blocks of indeterminate tomato, drip-fed
- Control: standard NPK rotation, no calcium-source supplement
- Trial: 4 kg/donum soluCN at fruit-set + 2 kg/donum soluNOP at pre-flowering, alternating with the standard NPK

## Result

Marketable yield was **+18% over control** with **−62% blossom-end-rot rejects**. EC at root zone held steady at 4.2 — the difference came from delivered Ca, not from leaching.
