## Trial setup

Two adjacent dryland blocks, identical soil prep, sown 18 March. Block A: SARDI 10 (original). Block B: SARDI 10 Series 2. Both at 22 kg/ha, AgriCote coating.

## Result

By the second cut, Series 2 was carrying **+11% dry-matter yield** and noticeably better leaf retention on stem — consistent with the Barenbrug datasheet's improved-persistence claim. Worth the seed-cost premium on this farm's economics.
