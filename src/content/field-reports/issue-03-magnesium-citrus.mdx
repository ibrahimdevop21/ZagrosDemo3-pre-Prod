## Symptom pattern

Lower-canopy leaves showed the classic citrus magnesium pattern — interveinal chlorosis with a green inverted-V at the leaf base. Leaf-tissue Mg measured 0.18% (deficient: &lt;0.25%).

## Protocol

Two foliar sprays of EPSO Top at 2% (20 g/L) solution, 14 days apart, applied early morning. EPSO Top dissolves cleanly without leaving deposits on fruit — important for the export-grade block under trial.

## Result

Chlorosis cleared in 21 days. Leaf-tissue Mg climbed to 0.31% by day 30. The block went on to produce **+8% Class 1 fruit** at harvest versus the prior season's untreated baseline.
