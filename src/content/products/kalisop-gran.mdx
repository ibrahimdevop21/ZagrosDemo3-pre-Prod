## Positioning

Same composition as KaliSOP fine (50% K₂O + 45% SO₃), in uniform granular form for easy spreading and dissolution. Applied throughout the growing season. EU Organic certified (EU 2018/848 + EC 889/2008).

## Benefits

- Uniform granule size for accurate, even spreading
- Quality enhancement and crop stability
- Resistance to pests, disease, heat, and salt stress
- K + S together improve nitrogen-use efficiency
- Sourced from natural raw potash salt
- Allowed in organic farming under EU regulation
- Chloride-free — safe on chloride-sensitive crops

## Application notes

Application rates use **feddan** units (~0.42 ha), distinct from the KaliSOP fine grade which uses kg/ha. Normalize per region for the website.

## Tank-mix notes

Designed primarily for solid-soil application via spreader. Run a tank-mix test before any new combination.
