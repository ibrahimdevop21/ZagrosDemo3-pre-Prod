## Positioning

Highest possible P and K concentrations in a single soluble product. Use via fertigation or foliar. Acidic buffer effect boosts application efficacy. Low EC = safe for salt-sensitive crops and small orchards.

## Benefits

- Top-tier fruit quality: sizing, hardness, color, sugar formation, shelf life
- High compatibility with most fertilizers and plant protection products
- Acidic buffer improves nutrient availability
- Low EC — safe on salt-sensitive crops

## Tank-mix cautions

Caution when mixing with Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺ — run a tank-mix test first. **DO NOT MIX with:** Calcium Nitrate (soluCN).
