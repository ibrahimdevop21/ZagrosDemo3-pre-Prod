## Positioning

A **winter-activity-10 lucerne** bred for top-end forage production in Mediterranean climates with winter-dominant rainfall. The Series 2 release improves persistence and leaf retention over the original SARDI 10, while keeping the rapid winter growth that suits dairy, hay, and silage systems.

## Key features

- Winter activity 10
- Excellent seedling vigor
- Excellent pest and disease tolerance
- Improved persistence over SARDI 10 and other highly winter-active cultivars
- Top-end forage production
- Improved leaf retention on stem (vs other highly winter-active cultivars) → better forage quality

## Target segments

- Specialist year-round hay production systems
- Short-term cropping rotations (3-5 years)
- High-intensity systems such as dairy

## Product fit

Like its predecessor SARDI 10, this Series 2 cultivar suits dryland mixed farming in Mediterranean climates with winter-dominant rainfall. As a highly winter-active variety, SARDI 10 Series 2 maximizes production during winter months that typically receive most rainfall. Quick regrowth makes it valuable for intensively irrigated systems including dairy farms and high-yield hay/silage production.
