## Positioning

"Magnesium & Sulphur — to Close Nutrient Gaps." Organic-certified Mg + S source (EU 2018/848 + EC 889/2008) for closing the K-Mg-N nutrient gap.

## Benefits

- **Mg** — drives chlorophyll production (controls fruit color), supports carbon fixation (CO₂ → carbohydrates), photosynthesis, protein building, and organic acid action; required for plant defense against fungal disease
- **S** — synergizes with N for amino acid balance; essential cofactor for phytohormones; protein building; stimulates beneficial soil microbes (legume N fixation)
- Balanced K-Mg-N application improves uptake of remaining elements (K, P)
- EU Organic certified — usable in certified organic operations

## K-Mg balance rule

When applying potassium, balance the K-Mg ratio at **1-2 : 1 (K:Mg)** to avoid antagonism.

## Tank-mix cautions

**DO NOT MIX with:** Calcium Nitrate (soluCN), phosphate sources (soluUP, soluMAP, soluMKP). Any P + Ca + Mg combination forms precipitates.
