## Positioning

Two key macronutrients (N + S) in immediately available form. Ideal for sulfur-responsive crops (vegetables, fruits, oilseeds). Non-caking, recommended during vegetative growth. Best on neutral-to-alkaline soils — the acidifying effect stimulates phosphate and micronutrient (Fe, Mn, Zn) uptake.

## Benefits

- Drives protein synthesis and vegetative growth
- Acidifies alkaline soil locally → unlocks phosphate and micronutrients
- Non-caking storage
- Provides immediately available ammoniacal N and sulphate S together

## Tank-mix cautions

Mixes with all water-soluble fertilizers **EXCEPT calcium-containing** (soluCN and any Ca source). **DO NOT MIX with pesticides.** Run a tank-mix test before any new combination.
