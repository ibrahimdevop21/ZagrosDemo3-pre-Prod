## Positioning

Acidic NP source for pivot irrigation, fertigation, and foliar application. Ideal from planting through flowering. Safe for high-pH soils and irrigation water (pH > 6.5). Keeps pivot pipes and drippers clean — prevents clogging.

soluUP is manufactured by Beluckey & Wengfu Technology Co. Ltd. (Chengdu, China) and distributed by K+S.

## Benefits

- Excellent N + P source for pivot, fertigation, foliar
- Improves uptake of phosphorus and micronutrients
- Acidic (pH 1.6–2.0 in 1% solution) — neutralizes alkaline irrigation water
- Keeps irrigation infrastructure clean

## Application notes

- Foliar — early veg: 0.5–1.0% (5–10 g/L)
- Foliar — flowering to fruiting: 1.0–2.0% (10–20 g/L)
- Minimum 250 L of water per feddan per application

## Tank-mix cautions

**DO NOT MIX with:** Calcium Nitrate (soluCN), Magnesium Sulphate (EPSO Top). Always run a tank-mix test before use. Store in a cool, dry place.
