## Positioning

Premium chloride-free, sodium-free K source — ideal for **salinity-sensitive** crops and saline soil/water situations. Essential where chloride is toxic. Suitable for greenhouse, open field, and hydroponics. Near-neutral pH.

## Benefits

- Increases fruit size, reduces fruit cracking, improves quality (foliar post-flowering)
- Reduces plant chloride uptake when toxic Cl is present
- Boosts sugar transport and formation → higher yield, better quality, longer shelf life
- Improves abiotic and biotic stress resistance (drought, frost, heat, pests, disease)
- Chloride- and sodium-free — safe for salinity-sensitive crops

## Safety

soluNOP is classified **UN 1486 / Class 5.1 (oxidizer)**. Store separately from organic materials, fuels, and reducing agents. UN packing code: 5H4/Y26/S/19/NL/B+K 3286.

## Tank-mix notes

Highly compatible with most water-soluble fertilizers. Run a tank-mix test before any new combination.
