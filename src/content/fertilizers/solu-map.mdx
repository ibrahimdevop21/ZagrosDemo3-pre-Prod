## Positioning

High-phosphorus starter fertilizer for early-stage root development. Ideal at planting through flowering. Standard high-P MAP grade — expected use cases mirror soluUP but with higher P density.

## Benefits

- Highest available P (~61%) concentration for early root and shoot development
- Acidic profile suits high-pH soils
- Compatible with most water-soluble fertilizers (test first)

## Tank-mix cautions

Same precaution profile as soluMKP and soluUP: caution with Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺. **DO NOT MIX with:** Calcium Nitrate (soluCN). Run a tank-mix test before any new combination.
