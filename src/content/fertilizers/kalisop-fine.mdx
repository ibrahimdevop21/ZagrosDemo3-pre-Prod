## Positioning

Highly concentrated SOP (sulphate of potash) — premium chloride-free K + S in fine powder form. Ideal for high-pH soils and high-value cash crops with low salt tolerance. Applicable across the full crop cycle. EU Organic certified (EU 2018/848 + EC 889/2008).

## Benefits

- Quality enhancement and crop stability
- Resistance to pests, disease, heat, and salt stress
- K + S together improve nitrogen-use efficiency
- Sourced from natural raw potash salt
- Allowed in organic farming under EU regulation
- Chloride-free — safe on chloride-sensitive crops (tobacco, grapes, citrus, berries)

## Tank-mix notes

Highly compatible with most water-soluble fertilizers and plant protection products. Run a tank-mix test before any new combination.
