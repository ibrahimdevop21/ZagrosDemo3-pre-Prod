# Homepage Implementation Plan (Plan 2 of 5)

**Spec reference:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-05-11-zagros-redesign-design.md` §6.1 "Home — The Field Report cover" and §5 Component inventory.

**Goal:** Deliver the editorial homepage as the first scroll-experience of the pitch. Establishes the Field-Report concept, surfaces the catalog one click away, and lets the agency case rest on actual design discipline rather than stock-template tropes.

**Dependencies on Plan 1 (already landed, tag `plan-1-foundation`):**
- Design tokens, paper-grain body, IBM Plex + Fraunces self-hosted fonts
- Layout.astro / Nav / Footer / CaptionStrip
- Products collection (14 SKUs) + tank-mix compatibility data
- Hand-curated EN/AR i18n strings
- Placeholder pages on the new Layout

**Out of scope (deferred to Plan 3+):**
- Products index / detail pages
- Field-report archive page (Plan 4)
- Partners directory page (Plan 4)
- About / Contact / Privacy / Terms (Plan 5)
- Real client photography — Plan 2 ships with clearly-marked illustrative imagery
- Native-Arabic editor pass — flagged in Plan 1, still pending

---

```mdx
## Background

Blossom-end rot pressure climbs whenever EC pushes past 3.5 and the
calcium pathway closes. The block under trial was carrying EC 4.2
from late-stage irrigation salinity — a textbook case for the
nitrate-only feeding pattern soluCN and soluNOP are built for.

## Trial setup

- Two equally-sized blocks of indeterminate tomato, drip-fed
- Control: standard NPK rotation, no calcium-source supplement
- Trial: 4 kg/donum soluCN at fruit-set + 2 kg/donum soluNOP at
  pre-flowering, alternating with the standard NPK

## Result

Marketable yield was **+18% over control** with **−62% blossom-end-rot
rejects**. EC at root zone held steady at 4.2 — the difference came
from delivered Ca, not from leaching.

> _Illustrative trial — pending audit by the agronomy desk before
> publication._
```
