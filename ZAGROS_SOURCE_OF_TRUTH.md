## 1. BRAND IDENTITY (inferred)

**Name:** Zagros (زاغروس) — likely named after the Zagros Mountains spanning
Iraq, Iran, and Turkey.

**Business model:** Authorized **distributor** of internationally branded
agricultural inputs in the Middle East. Zagros is NOT a manufacturer — it
sources from established global brands and serves the regional ag market.

**Two product lines:**
1. **Fertilizers** — distributed under the **K+S Group** brand
   (Germany), supplied through **K Plus S Middle East FZE DMCC** (Dubai).
   The bull mark seen on every fertilizer bag is the K+S logo.
2. **Forage seeds** — distributed under the **Barenbrug** brand
   (Netherlands; major global forage seed company), with cultivars from
   Australian and American breeding programs.

**Visual language (from materials):**
- K+S bull logo present on every fertilizer
- Color-coding by product line:
  - soluNPK / soluUP: dark navy blue
  - soluMKP: teal
  - soluMAP: green/teal (varies)
  - soluAMS Premium: deep red/burgundy
  - soluNOP: mint green
  - soluCN: white/light green
  - EPSO Top: green (organic)
  - KaliSOP: blue (royal blue)
- Barenbrug: yellow/black accent with green-dominated photography;
  category color stripes (purple = Lucerne/Alfalfa, green = Tropical)
- Typography: clean, technical, agronomic (not consumer/lifestyle)

### 3.1 soluUP — NP 17-44-00 (Monoammonium Phosphate, acidic)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Product code** | soluUP |
| **Type** | EC Fertiliser, NP |
| **Grade** | 17-44-0 |
| **N** | 17% Total Nitrogen (Amide form) |
| **P₂O₅** | 44% water-soluble |
| **pH (1% sol.)** | 1.6-2.0 (typical 1.9) — **acidic** |
| **Bag size** | 25 kg |
| **Origin** | China (Beluckey & Wengfu) |

**Positioning:** Acidic NP source for pivot irrigation, fertigation, and
foliar application. Ideal from planting through flowering. Safe for
high-pH soils and irrigation water (pH > 6.5). Keeps pivot pipes and
drippers clean — prevents clogging.

**Benefits:**
- Excellent N + P source for pivot, fertigation, foliar
- Improves uptake of phosphorus and micronutrients
- Acidic — neutralizes alkaline irrigation water

**Application rates:**

| Method | Rate |
|---|---|
| Foliar — early veg | 0.5-1.0% (5-10 g/L) |
| Foliar — flowering to fruiting | 1.0-2.0% (10-20 g/L) |
| Fertigation — early stages | 1.5-2.5 kg/feddan |
| Fertigation — flowering to fruiting | up to 5 kg/feddan |
| Min water/feddan/application | 250 L |

**Crop-specific:**
- **Field crops** (wheat, corn, sorghum, cotton, sesame, groundnut):
  2-3 applications at 10-15 day intervals, 30-60 days after planting
- **Vegetables** (tomato, potato, onion, cucumber): 2.5-5.0 kg/feddan at
  planting, flowering, early fruit dev, and/or after 2nd harvest
- **Fruit trees** (citrus, mango, banana): 1-2 applications × 5-10
  kg/feddan pre- and post-flowering
- **Forages** (alfalfa, grasses): pivot every 7-10 days at 2.5-5.0
  kg/feddan during initial growth; repeat after alternate cutting

**⚠️ DO NOT MIX with:** Calcium Nitrate (soluCN), Magnesium Sulphate (EPSO Top).
Always run tank-mix test before use.

**Storage:** Cool, dry place.

### 3.8 soluCN — Calcium Nitrate

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Product code** | soluCN |
| **Type** | Calcium Nitrate, water-soluble |
| **N** | 15.5% min |
| **Ca** | 19.0% min |
| **Bag size** | 25 kg |

**Positioning:** The **only water-soluble calcium source**. Easily
absorbed via roots and foliage. Use in greenhouses and open fields with
all irrigation systems. Critical for calcium-deficiency disorders.

**Benefits:**
- Prevents/treats blossom-end rot, nail-head spot, fruit cracking
- Improves fruit quality and storage life (fruits, tubers, bulbs)
- Reduces soil salinity when applied via drip
- Improves soil structure; reduces Na and Mg toxicity
- Boosts photosynthesis efficiency; activates enzymes and meristematic tissues
- Aids fertilization, reduces flower/fruit drop after set
- Encourages root growth, protects from rot, improves heat tolerance

**Application rates:**

| Crop type | Apps × Rate | Stage |
|---|---|---|
| Vegetables (tomato, potato, onion, cucurbits) | 2-3 × 4-5 kg/donum | Late flowering, early fruit, after 2nd cut |
| Fruit (mango, citrus, banana) | 2-3 × 7.5-10 kg/donum | Pre-flowering, post-flowering, early fruit |
| Forages (clover, grasses) | 7.5-15 kg/donum | Soil or pivot |
| Field crops (corn, wheat, cotton) | 3 × 5-6 kg/donum | Planting, vegetative, flowering |
| Oilseeds (sunflower, peanut) | 2 × 5-7.5 kg/donum | Peg formation, pod development |

**Calcium deficiency note:** Ca is **immobile** in the plant —
concentrated in leaves, stems, roots — so deficiency symptoms appear
at growing tips: drying tips on shoots and roots, dead spots on leaves,
blossom-end rot on fruits, drying of new leaf edges followed by
twisting and dieback.

**⚠️ DO NOT MIX with:** soluUP (MAP/MKP-style P fertilizers) and
EPSO Top (Mg sulphate).

**Product narrative:** Bred in Australia from the market-leading Tolgar
Rhodes, retaining its strengths while improving forage quality. Thinner
leaf blades produce finer-textured vegetative growth with reduced stem.
Enhanced multiple tillering yields a higher leaf-to-stem ratio →
greater consumable dry matter than Tolgar Rhodes and other Katambora
Rhodes grasses. Conditioning and dry-down significantly improved → less
stem, more dry-down rate, faster utilization of leaf dry-down gains.
Maturity evenness yields higher protein feed later in the season at
higher utilization rates. Exceptional persistence and recovery after
grazing or cutting. Later flowering than Tolgar Rhodes maintains feed
quality longer.

**Agronomy detail:** Suited to tropical, sub-tropical, and (with
conditions) temperate zones. Wide soil adaptation: infertile sands to
fertile loams to Brigalow clays. Requires adequate nutrition and some
form of rotational grazing for medium-to-long-term persistence. Diploid
Rhodes establishes and persists well even at low fertility, with higher
drought tolerance and faster growth than tetraploid Rhodes types. Can
struggle on heavy cracking clays. Moderate frost tolerance, better than
tetraploid Rhodes.

## 6. COMPATIBILITY MATRIX (CRITICAL — for tank mixing copy)

| Product | DO NOT MIX WITH |
|---|---|
| **soluUP** | soluCN (Calcium Nitrate), EPSO Top (Mg Sulphate) |
| **soluCN** | Phosphate sources (soluUP, soluMAP, soluMKP), EPSO Top |
| **EPSO Top** | soluCN, soluUP (any P + Ca + Mg combo) |
| **soluAMS Premium** | Calcium-containing fertilizers, all pesticides |
| **soluMKP** | Caution with Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺ — tank-mix test first |
| **soluMAP** | Same as MKP/UP — caution with Ca and Mg |

**Universal rule:** Always perform a small **tank-mix compatibility
test** before any new combination. Watch for precipitation, color
change, or temperature change.

**Why these incompatibilities exist (for educational copy):**
- Ca²⁺ + phosphate → calcium phosphate precipitate (clogs drippers)
- Ca²⁺ + sulphate → calcium sulphate precipitate (gypsum)
- Ammonium + free lime/Ca → ammonia gas loss
