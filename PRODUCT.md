# Zagros Trading — Product Context

**Register:** brand. This is a marketing publication for a Sudanese B2B agricultural-inputs distributor. The website *is* the brand surface, not a tool the user operates.

## Users

Three audiences. Ranked by traffic value, not headcount.

1. **Sudanese commercial farm operators** — Alrajihi, Dal, Paramount, Premier, Amtar-scale projects + smaller commercial farms. Multilingual (AR primary, some EN). Browse for fertilizers, seeds, pesticides by crop and stage. Care about supplier credentials, application rates, tank-mix compatibility.
2. **Ministry of Agriculture & state agriculture buyers** — procurement teams sourcing inputs at scale. Need trust signals heavy (certifications, supplier-network depth, branch coverage).
3. **Smaller farmers & cooperatives** — find products on WhatsApp / phone after browsing. Less digital-fluent. Mobile-first. Tap-to-call critical.

Zagros's customers are not consumers. They're agriculture professionals. Voice should be technical, agronomically credible, never "DTC friendly."

## Product purpose

Zagros Trading is a regional MENA distributor of agricultural inputs, headquartered in Sudan and active across twelve markets — Sudan (primary), Egypt, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, Jordan, Lebanon, Iraq, and Yemen. The company sources premium seeds, fertilizers, and pesticides from world-leading manufacturers across Europe, Asia, and the Middle East. Established 1987 in Khartoum. Six Sudan branches: Khartoum HQ (Bahry industrial zone) + Port Sudan, Al Qadarif, Al Managil, Ad-Damar, Ad-Daba. ~50 staff. Member of the International Agricultural Trade Association.

Three product lines: Seeds (Barenbrug AU forage + East West TH vegetable, catalog pending), Fertilizers (11 K+S SKUs from Germany), Pesticides (16 SKUs from SAF, KZ, Kafr El Zayat, and ~12 Chinese chemistry partners). Seven international supplier partnerships across six countries.

Differentiators: 15-year track record, six-branch national network, full input portfolio (only Sudanese distributor stocking all three lines), ministry-grade trust.

## Tone

- Technical, B2B, agronomically literate. Spec-forward (N-P-K numbers, AI %, application rates).
- Bilingual AR-first, EN-secondary. Hand-translated, never machine-translated. AR uses Arabic-Indic numerals for visible AR copy; ISO codes and phone digits stay Western.
- Editorial publication voice — closer to *Bloomberg* or a regional ag-trade journal than to "DTC SaaS landing page."
- AR vocabulary discipline: lucerne = البرسيم الحجازي (not جرجير), fertigation = تسميد بالري, pesticide = مبيد, fertilizer = سماد, forage = أعلاف.
- Trust signals heavy: supplier logos with flags, customer references by name, branch network specifics.

## Anti-references (what we are not)

- **Not a generic AI-SaaS landing page.** No icons-in-circles trio, no "build / ship / scale" trichotomy, no purple/blue gradients, no abstract waveform hero illustrations.
- **Not "DTC ag-tech."** Not chatty. Not emoji-loaded. Not "your friend in agriculture." Customers are professionals; the brand respects that.
- **Not a manufacturer.** Zagros sources from suppliers — never imply we make anything.
- **Not "operator, not a brand house."** Zagros IS the brand customers buy. Suppliers are credentials. This invented framing got purged in Plan 6.
- **Not a regional MENA distributor.** Sudan-foreground. K+S Middle East FZE is a legitimate supplier company name — but that's the only "Middle East" reference the brand uses.
- **Not stock-photo-y.** Field photos must be real Sudan agriculture. Placeholder labeled tiles are honest gaps, plausible stock photos are dishonest.

## Strategic principles

1. **Do not invent content.** Every fact must be in `ZAGROS_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`. Unknown facts render as visible `[pending]` markers, never plausible filler.
2. **Editorial, not SaaS.** Magazine-page rhythm. Generous spacing. Asymmetric grids. Big type. Real numbers. Photo placeholders honestly labeled until client provides real assets.
3. **Sudan-foreground.** Branches first, supplier credentials second. No aspirational regional claims.
4. **Mobile-first, RTL-equal.** Sudanese farmers browse on phones with limited bandwidth. Performance budget: <100KB compressed JS first load, LCP <2.5s on 3G. RTL parity at every viewport.
5. **44px tap targets everywhere.** WCAG 2.5.5. Tap-to-call and tap-to-WhatsApp are first-class CTAs.
6. **Restrained editorial motion.** Animation as acknowledgment, not spectacle. Always reduced-motion safe. Hardware-accelerated (transform + opacity) only.
